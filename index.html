Field note

A lift-and-shift rule migration isn't a deployment.

Copying port-based rules onto a new firewall leaves App-ID, User-ID and threat profiles switched off. Customers end up paying for capability they never use, and renewals get hard. We deploy with the platform's controls turned on and ownership clearly handed over.
